PS – Polystyrene (Resin Code #6)

Properties

  • Thickness: 0.3mm – 1mm
  • Rigid, transparent, and lightweight
  • Poor resistance to UV and heat

Common Uses

  • Disposable cups and plates
  • Foam packaging and insulation
  • CD cases and plastic cutlery

Recyclability

  • ♻️ Difficult to recycle due to low density
  • Often ends up in landfills or oceans

Hazards: Can release toxic styrene when burned — harmful to health and air quality.
