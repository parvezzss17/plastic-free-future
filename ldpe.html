LDPE – Low-Density Polyethylene (Resin Code #4)

Properties

  • Thickness: 0.1mm – 0.5mm
  • Flexible, soft, and lightweight
  • Moisture-resistant but has low heat resistance

Common Uses

  • Plastic bags and cling wraps
  • Squeeze bottles
  • Flexible tubing and film packaging

Recyclability

  • ♻️ Recyclable in some regions
  • Often reused for garbage can liners and floor tiles

Hazards: Burning LDPE can release harmful gases and microplastics.
