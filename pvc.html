PVC – Polyvinyl Chloride (Resin Code #3)

Properties

  • Thickness: 0.5mm – 2.5mm
  • Strong, rigid or flexible depending on formulation
  • Good electrical insulation and chemical resistance

Common Uses

  • Pipes, window frames, and flooring
  • Medical tubing and vinyl products
  • Credit cards and synthetic leather

Recyclability

  • ♻️ Recyclable but not commonly accepted in all recycling centers
  • Requires special processing to avoid chlorine release

Hazards: Burning PVC releases dioxins — one of the most toxic environmental pollutants.
