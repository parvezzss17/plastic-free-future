PET – Polyethylene Terephthalate (Resin Code #1)

Properties

  • Thickness: 0.2mm – 2mm
  • Strong, lightweight, and transparent
  • Resistant to moisture and chemicals

Common Uses

  • Beverage bottles
  • Food packaging trays
  • Microwave-safe containers

Recyclability

  • ♻️ Highly recyclable
  • Used to make fibers, carpets, and clothing

Hazards: Reusing PET bottles may release harmful chemicals when heated.
