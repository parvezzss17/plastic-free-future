HDPE – High-Density Polyethylene (Resin Code #2)

Properties

  • Thickness: 0.4mm – 3mm
  • Strong, durable, and resistant to impact
  • Excellent resistance to chemicals and moisture

Common Uses

  • Milk jugs and detergent bottles
  • Pipes and plastic lumber
  • Containers for household chemicals

Recyclability

  • ♻️ Highly recyclable and reused in non-food applications
  • Recycled HDPE can be used for piping and plastic furniture

Hazards: Burning HDPE releases toxic fumes. Always recycle responsibly.
