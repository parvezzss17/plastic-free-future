PP – Polypropylene (Resin Code #5)

Properties

  • Thickness: 0.3mm – 2mm
  • Lightweight, tough, and heat resistant
  • Excellent fatigue and chemical resistance

Common Uses

  • Food containers and bottle caps
  • Ropes, car parts, and textiles
  • Stationery and reusable products

Recyclability

  • ♻️ Recyclable and often reused for bins, trays, and auto parts

Hazards: Improper disposal may cause microplastic pollution.
